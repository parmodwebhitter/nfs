What we looked at

What makes agency visitors become leads

We reviewed the homepages of large agencies and our direct market (Web Hitters, Brandwitty, WebFX, Ignite Visibility, Thrive and PageTraffic) for the patterns they share. Seven points come up again and again on the sites that are built to convert:

One clear offer above the fold

A specific outcome in the headline and one low-effort action, usually a free audit or proposal, with the form or button visible without scrolling.

Proof straight after the hero

Star ratings, review counts and client logos within the first screen and a half. Visitors look for trust before they read services.

Numbers, not adjectives

Results such as "+212% leads in 6 months" outperform claims like "best agency". Every proof section should carry a figure.

A call to action every 2–3 sections

Sticky header button, mid-page audit band and a final form. On mobile: a sticky call / WhatsApp bar, which matters a lot in the Indian market.

Short forms

3–4 fields convert far better than long forms. Website URL + email is enough to start an audit; qualify later by phone.

Remove the risk

"Free audit", "no lock-in contract", "reply within 24 hours" next to every form reduce hesitation.

Show the price range

Starting-from packages (as Web Hitters does) pre-qualify leads and stop visitors leaving to find pricing elsewhere.

ChatGPT & Bing

How we get NFS Digital recommended by AI search

ChatGPT search relies heavily on Bing's index (alongside OpenAI's own crawler), so ranking on Bing and being cited by ChatGPT go together. The plan:

1

Get indexed by Bing fast

Verify the site in Bing Webmaster Tools, submit the sitemap, enable IndexNow so new pages are picked up within hours, and list the business on Bing Places.

2

Let the AI crawlers in

robots.txt allows Bingbot, OAI-SearchBot (ChatGPT search), GPTBot, PerplexityBot and others. Done in the draft site.

3

Content that is easy to quote

Question-style headings with a direct 40–60 word answer underneath, comparison tables, and figures with sources. Plain HTML so content is readable without JavaScript.

4

Structured data

Organization / ProfessionalService and FAQ schema on the homepage (done in the draft), plus Service, Review and Article schema on inner pages.

5

Be mentioned elsewhere

AI answers lean on third-party sources: Clutch, GoodFirms, Google reviews, LinkedIn, industry "top agencies" lists and press mentions. Keep the name, address and phone identical everywhere.

6

Measure it

Track visits from chatgpt.com, copilot.microsoft.com and perplexity.ai in GA4, and run a fixed set of test prompts each month (e.g. "best SEO agency in Delhi") to see whether NFS is named.

Also added: an llms.txt summary file. It is a new, optional convention; no major AI search engine has confirmed using it yet, so treat it as low-cost, not a ranking factor.
